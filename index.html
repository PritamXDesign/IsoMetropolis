🏗️ City Building Core Rules

1. Road Networks: Zones must have road access to develop. Roads autotile into straightaways, corners, T-junctions, and 4-way crossings. Building roads over water creates suspension bridges!

2. Power & Water: Zones require both electricity (wind, solar, coal) and water (water tower, river pump). Utilities conduct through roads and adjacent structures.

3. RCI Balance: Keep Residential, Commercial, and Industrial zones balanced to avoid unemployment or goods shortages.

4. Public Services: Build Police Stations, Fire Departments, Clinics, and Schools to raise land value and prevent disasters.

5. Architectural Styles: IsoMetropolis lets you choose and mix styles freely! Switch between European Haussmann, Modern Metropolis, Neo-Tokyo, Cyberpunk, and Medieval anytime.
